# DevCompass - UI Design and Theme Overview

This document outlines the design system and visual identity for the DevCompass project. The design philosophy is rooted in the traditional Japanese concept of Kanban, focusing on clarity, efficient workflow organization, and a calm visual experience.

## Brand Identity and Theme

- **Concept Inspiration:** Based on the Japanese Kanban methodology, emphasizing clear structure, minimalist layout, and well-balanced content hierarchy.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Incorporates subtle Japanese design touches, including traditional color harmonies, seal-like branding elements, and generous whitespace.
- **Color Distribution Ratio:** 60% Washi Cream (`--bg`) for comfortable reading, 30% Deep Pine (`--secondary`) for structure and contrast, and 10% Vermilion (`--primary`) for primary actions and key highlights.

### Color Palette (`src/index.css`)

- **Primary:** `--primary: #C8372D;` (Vermilion)
- **Secondary:** `--secondary: #2E4A3B;` and `--secondary-deep: #213528;` (Deep Pine)
- **Backgrounds and Surfaces:** `--bg: #F6F1E7;`, `--surface: #FFFCF6;`, `--surface-alt: #E9DFCB;`, `--border: #DDD3C2;`
- **Accents:** `--gold: #C79A3B;`, `--sakura: #F4D9D4;`

## Typography

- **Headings:** *"Shippori Mincho"*, serif (Provides a refined traditional aesthetic).
- **Body and UI:** *"Zen Kaku Gothic New"*, sans-serif (Ensures clear readability across interface elements).
- **Code:** *"JetBrains Mono"*, monospace.
